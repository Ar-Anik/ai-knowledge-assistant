Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 20 annual leave days every year.

Employees can apply for annual leave through the employee portal.

Leave applications should normally be submitted before the leave starts.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Employees should contact the HR department if they have questions about leave policy.

Company Working Hours

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.

Employees should inform their manager if they expect to be late.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely when remote work is approved by their manager.

Remote work requests should be submitted through the employee portal.

Employees working remotely must remain available during normal working hours.
